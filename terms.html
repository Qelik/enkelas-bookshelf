Terms & Community Rules

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Enkela's Bookshelf is a reading tracker. Most of it runs entirely on your own device and needs no account at all. Two parts are shared with other people — the Community board, where readers recommend books, and Reading clubs, where a handful of friends discuss one book together. These rules cover those shared parts, and they apply to everyone who signs in.

Your account and your data

  • An account is optional. Without one, your books never leave your device.
  • With one, your bookshelf syncs privately between your own devices. Nobody else can read it — not other users, and it isn't sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • You can delete your account at any time, from within the app. Deleting is immediate and permanent: your bookshelf, your recommendations and your club comments are erased. Export a backup first if you want to keep your reading history.
  • Keep your password to yourself, and use one you don't use anywhere else.

No objectionable content — zero tolerance

Anything you post to the Community board or to a reading club must not be:

  • harassment, threats, or abuse directed at another person;
  • hate speech or slurs targeting anyone's race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, or sexual orientation;
  • sexual content involving minors, or sexually explicit material generally;
  • content promoting violence or self-harm;
  • spam, advertising, affiliate links, or scams;
  • someone else's private information;
  • deliberate spoilers posted outside the progress point they belong to.

Accounts that post this material are removed. There is no warning system and no appeal queue — this is a small book app run by one person, and the bar for staying is simply not doing the above.

Reporting something

Every recommendation and every club comment has a Report action. Reports are reviewed, and objectionable content and the accounts responsible are removed within 24 hours.

Content reported by several different readers is hidden automatically, straight away, without waiting for that review — so the worst posts come down in minutes rather than hours.

You can also block any reader. Blocking is immediate and private: their posts stop appearing for you everywhere in the app, they are never told, and nothing changes for anyone else.

The boring necessary part

  • You keep ownership of what you write. By posting to the Community board or a reading club you allow it to be shown to other readers of the app — nothing more.
  • Book covers and details come from Open Library and Google Books, and belong to their respective owners.
  • The app is provided as-is, with no warranty. It's a free reading tracker made for the love of it. Keep your own backups — ⚙️ Settings → Export takes a second.
  • These rules may change; the date at the top says when they last did.
